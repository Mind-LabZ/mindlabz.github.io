How a project runs

  1. Concept

    We shape your idea, or pitch you ours, and write a design doc everyone builds from.

  2. Prototype

    A playable build in days, so you judge the core loop by playing it.

  3. Production

    Engineering, art, levels, onboarding and sound, with regular builds you can play.

  4. Launch

    Store page, ad creatives and test campaigns, then release on Google Play and the App Store.

  5. Live ops

    Updates, events and tuning based on what your players actually do.
